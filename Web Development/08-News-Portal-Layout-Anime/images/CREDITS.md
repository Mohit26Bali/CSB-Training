# Image credits

## Saved images (offline snapshot)

`images/saved/` holds 74 images (about 1476 KB) saved on 8 October 2026 so the site works offline:

- **Cover art and banners** (`cover-*.webp`, `banner-*.webp`): from AniList. They belong to the studios and publishers of each show. Each show links to its AniList page.
- **News images** (`news-*.webp`): from Crunchyroll News and MyAnimeList. They belong to those publishers, and each story links to the original article.

When the site is online, these images load directly from AniList and the publishers instead.

## Trailer thumbnails

Show pages load trailer thumbnails from YouTube (`i.ytimg.com`) and link to the trailer on YouTube.
