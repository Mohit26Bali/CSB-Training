Big, round leaves with silver stripes make the Orbifolia one of the most beautiful foliage plants you can grow indoors.

It's a little fussier than most: it loves humidity and dislikes tap water and draughts. Keep it happy and it rewards you with huge, glossy leaves.

  • Safe for cats and dogs
  • Leaves gently move during the day
  • Ideal for bright bathrooms with natural humidity
