Heart-shaped leaves splashed with gold trail from shelves and hanging pots, or climb up a moss pole if you prefer.

Pothos tells you when it's thirsty by drooping slightly, then perks up within hours of a drink, which makes it perfect for first-time plant owners.

  • Trails or climbs, so it suits shelves and hanging planters
  • Grows fast in good light
  • Easy to propagate from cuttings in water
