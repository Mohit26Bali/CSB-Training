Questions

Good to know

What is included in the price?

Return flights from London, all hotels, the transport and activities listed on each trip, and a local guide where shown. Travel insurance is not included.

Can I change the trip?

Yes. Every package can be adjusted: add nights, upgrade hotels or fly from another airport. Send an enquiry and an expert will reply within one working day.

How much do I pay to book?

A 20% deposit holds your place. The rest is due 10 weeks before you travel.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. We send a checklist for your destination when you book, but always check the official government advice before you travel.
