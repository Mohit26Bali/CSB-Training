New to climbing?

Your first climb in three easy steps

Bouldering is climbing on short walls without ropes, over thick crash mats. It is the easiest way to start climbing, and most people can do it.

  1. 1

    Book a taster

    A 60-minute session with a coach. Entry and shoe hire are included, so all you bring is yourself.

  2. 2

    Learn the basics

    How to fall safely, read a problem by its colour and move your feet. It sounds like a lot, but it takes about 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Climb on your own

    After your taster you can come back any time with a day pass or membership. Problems are graded, so you always know where to start.

    FAQ

    Questions before your first visit

    Still not sure? Call us on 0114 496 0123 and we will talk you through it.

    Do I need any experience or equipment?

    No. A taster session teaches you everything you need, and shoes are included. Wear clothes you can stretch in, like leggings or tracksuit bottoms and a T-shirt.

    Is bouldering safe?

    The walls are no higher than 4.5 metres, and the floor is covered in thick crash mats. Like any sport there is some risk, which is why every first visit starts with a short safety talk on how to fall and where not to stand.

    What are the safety rules?
    • Never stand or sit on the mats under someone who is climbing.
    • Climb down when you can, rather than jumping from the top.
    • One climber per problem, and look before you start.
    • Under-16s must be supervised by an adult, except in Kids' Club.
    How old do children need to be?

    Children aged 4 and up can climb on the kids' wall with an adult. Kids' Club is for ages 6 to 11, and children can be dropped off.

    What do the colours mean?

    Each problem uses holds of one colour, and the colour shows how hard it is: white is the easiest, then yellow, green, blue, red and black. Most beginners start on white and yellow.

    Can I cancel or change a booking?

    Yes, for free, up to 24 hours before your session. Just reply to your confirmation email.
