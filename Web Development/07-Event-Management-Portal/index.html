The venue

Lumen Arts Centre has two cinema screens, a 400-seat main hall and a workshop studio, all under one roof.

Address
Lumen Arts Centre, 12 Neon Street, Manchester M1 4NF
Getting there
5 minutes' walk from Piccadilly station. No parking on site.
Access
Step-free throughout. Wheelchair spaces in every screen (choose them in the seat map), hearing loops and a quiet room.
Subtitles
All Japanese-language screenings have English subtitles.

Questions

Can I come in cosplay?

Yes, please do! Props must be light and not look like real weapons. There is a cloakroom for large props.

Are the screenings suitable for children?

The Saturday Totoro matinee is made for families. Other screenings follow their usual age ratings, which are shown at the box office.

What happens if an event is sold out?

Join the waiting list. If someone cancels, the next person on the list gets an email and has 24 hours to accept.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, up to 48 hours before the event. Cancel from "My tickets" and the seats go back on sale.
