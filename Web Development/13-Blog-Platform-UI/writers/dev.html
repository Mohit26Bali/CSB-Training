I trained as a chef in Chennai and Bristol before I realised I liked eating other people's cooking more than my own. I travel to learn how ordinary people eat on an ordinary day, which usually means markets at dawn and plastic stools at midnight.

I write about food with one rule: if I would not go back and pay for it again, it does not go in the story.
