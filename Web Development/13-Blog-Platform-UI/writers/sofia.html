I have not taken a short-haul flight since 2020. Instead I take night trains, regional trains and, once, a very slow cargo ferry. I write about how to travel well without spending much, and I keep receipts so you do not have to guess.

When I am not on a train I am in Bologna, eating tortellini and planning the next timetable puzzle.
