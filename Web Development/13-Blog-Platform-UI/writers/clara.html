I grew up above a bakery in Porto and have been trying to find a better pastel de nata ever since. I started Long Way Round in 2019 as a place to write the travel stories I wanted to read: slow, specific and honest about what went wrong.

These days I edit the journal from London, plan far too many walking routes and still believe the best way to see a city is to get up before it does.
