How to apply

You can apply before you have your exam results. It takes about 15 minutes.

On this page

  • The four steps

  • Fees and funding
  • Questions

The four steps

  1. Choose a course

    Search our courses, or come to an open evening and talk to a tutor.

  2. Apply online

    The form takes about 15 minutes. You do not need your exam results yet.

  3. Meet us

    We invite you to a short, friendly interview to check the course is right for you.

  4. Enrol

    Once you have your results, you enrol in August and start in September.

Start your application Prefer paper? Call us on 0113 496 0420 and we will post a form.

Entry requirements

These are typical requirements. If you do not meet them, talk to us: there is a course at every level.

Typical entry requirements by course level
Course levelWhat you usually need
Level 1No formal qualifications needed.
Level 2Usually 4 GCSEs at grade 3 or above, or a Level 1 course.
Level 3Usually 5 GCSEs at grade 4 or above, including English and maths.
Access to HEAged 19 or over, with GCSE English and maths at grade 4 (C) or above.

Fees and funding

Full-time courses are free if you are 16 to 18 on 31 August 2027. Apprenticeships are free to you, and you are paid. Adults may be able to get help with fees.

  • Bursary for ages 16 to 19

    Help with travel, equipment and meals if your household income is low.

  • Advanced Learner Loan

    Adults aged 19 and over can borrow the fees for Level 3 courses and above. It is written off if you complete a university degree.

  • Learning support

    Extra help for dyslexia, autism, mental health and physical disabilities. Tell us when you apply and we will plan it with you.

Questions about applying

Do I need my exam results to apply?

No. Apply now with the grades you expect, and we will confirm your place when your results arrive.

Is there a deadline?

Courses fill up from March, so apply by 31 March 2027 for September courses to be sure of an interview.

What if I do not get the grades?

We will help you find a course at the right level. Nobody who applies is left without an offer.

Can I study part-time while working?

Yes. Our evening courses run from 18:00 to 21:00, and apprenticeships combine work and study.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking at both campuses, plus bike storage. The Main Campus is a 5-minute walk from Fernhill station.
