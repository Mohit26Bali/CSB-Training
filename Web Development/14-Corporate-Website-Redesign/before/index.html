Welcome to Fernhill College

Fernhill College is a leading provider of world-class, learner-centred further education and training solutions, delivering innovative and flexible programmes of study across a diverse range of vocational and academic curriculum areas to over 4,200 learners each year. Established in 1964, we are committed to excellence and to empowering our learners to achieve their full potential through high quality teaching, outstanding facilities and strong partnerships with employers, ensuring that 93% of our learners progress to positive destinations including employment, apprenticeships and higher education. With over 120+ courses available at our Main Campus and Riverside Centre, there really is something for everyone at Fernhill!
