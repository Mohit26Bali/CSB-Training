How To Apply

Applying to Fernhill College couldn't be easier! Simply browse our extensive range of courses using our online course directory or by downloading a copy of our prospectus, then complete an application form, either online or by downloading the application form (Microsoft Word document, 2.3MB) and returning it to Student Services by post or in person. You do not need to have your exam results before applying. Once we have received your application you will be invited to attend an informal interview with a member of our teaching team, which is an opportunity for us to make sure the course is right for you. Following a successful interview you will receive a conditional offer, and once you have received your results you will be invited to enrol during our enrolment period in August, before starting your course in September. Courses fill up quickly from March onwards so we recommend that you apply by 31/03/2027 for September courses to guarantee an interview. If you do not achieve the grades you need we will help you to find an alternative course at the appropriate level.

Entry Requirements
Level 1No formal qualifications needed.
Level 2Usually 4 GCSEs at grade 3 or above, or a Level 1 course.
Level 3Usually 5 GCSEs at grade 4 or above, including English and maths.
Access to HEAged 19 or over, with GCSE English and maths at grade 4 (C) or above.
Financial Support

Bursary for ages 16 to 19: Help with travel, equipment and meals if your household income is low. Advanced Learner Loan: Adults aged 19 and over can borrow the fees for Level 3 courses and above. It is written off if you complete a university degree. Learning support: Extra help for dyslexia, autism, mental health and physical disabilities. Tell us when you apply and we will plan it with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need my exam results to apply?
A: No. Apply now with the grades you expect, and we will confirm your place when your results arrive.

Q: Is there a deadline?
A: Courses fill up from March, so apply by 31 March 2027 for September courses to be sure of an interview.

Q: What if I do not get the grades?
A: We will help you find a course at the right level. Nobody who applies is left without an offer.

Q: Can I study part-time while working?
A: Yes. Our evening courses run from 18:00 to 21:00, and apprenticeships combine work and study.

Q: Is there parking?
A: Yes, free parking at both campuses, plus bike storage. The Main Campus is a 5-minute walk from Fernhill station.
