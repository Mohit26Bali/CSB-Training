Our story

A shared table for every taste

Saffron & Sage began with a simple idea: friends should not have to agree on one cuisine to eat together. Our cooks bring recipes from Italy, India, Japan and the eastern Mediterranean into one kitchen, so a pizza lover, a curry fan and a ramen regular can all order what they love.

  • Wood-fired oven for pizza and flatbreads
  • Fresh, local produce delivered every morning
  • Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices in every course

Meet the chef

Chef Maya Fernandes

Maya trained in Florence, cooked in Mumbai and spent two years in a Tokyo ramen bar before opening Saffron & Sage. The menu brings those kitchens together, with each dish cooked the way it is cooked at home.

Good food travels well. I want every guest to taste a little of somewhere new, and still feel at home.
